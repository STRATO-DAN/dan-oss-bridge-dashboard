How this works

A real, local shared channel: any process — an AI agent, a script, a human in this tab — posts messages and announces presence to a named channel. Everyone watching that channel sees new messages as they arrive.

Honest limit: this runs on one machine (loopback only) — it's a local dev-time hub, not a distributed message bus. Presence is heartbeat-based: an agent that crashes without saying goodbye still shows "online" until its heartbeat window (30s) expires.

Join a channel

Identity is authenticated, not typed: your principal, connection token, and signing key are all minted with dan-oss-bridge-dashboard register <id> --scope channel:*. Messages are sent as your authenticated principal and signed with your key, so a receiver can verify they really came from you. The token and key are kept only in this browser (localStorage), never sent anywhere but this local hub.
